title: Halting

description: Time is not moving.

<Poem>{`
effective immediately
time will be halting
halted for faulting
and halted for good.

effective unstoppable
now that it's started
the halting we wanted
that would bear down in moments
we all understood

will stop with a shudder
like a cold locomotive
with no sand for the hourglass
or coal for the furnace
and each part that was moving
now still in the hood

that "now" will be tender
Now wasted on whether
each mendicant moment
or engine that could
unforgiven by halting
—that daunting dismember—
will be each shown their faces
their drivetrains and axles
volunteered to the never
for the first and last 'should.'

For the moment—no, madness—
(for moments are gone)
that the slowing creeps down
and the halting be done;
vindicated be humans
true will it become
that the madness is moment
is solitary 
has won

For the stillness that follows
or fails to follow
will have no moments prior
no reality done
and the certainty known
of that never before
will extend just as surely
to the time yet to come

so immediately proven
when the halting effects
the effect has no when
no before and no after
so then do I wonder

were the moments 
lost to madness
or did they never exist

or is the madness only proof
that the halting is wrong
and time is not moving
and never could
`}</Poem>
