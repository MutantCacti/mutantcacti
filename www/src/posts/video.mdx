title: Video

<Poem>{`
The window in my classroom
that I have so solely known before
frames a shock of pinecones near
and lines of branches in the background
curling into the uninterpretable
where three trees turn 
into fog and forest in the distance

and the winter that we thought was dying
has blessed another breath of snow
powdering the thinnest branches
of the nettled tree that brushes on the glass
which watching sway appears to me
as if framed by the ochre of the window
just like a video online
that looks too beautiful to be true

and then I imagine
that I have muted the snowfall out the window
to focus on the real world
but then remember when I walk outside
the snow will dust my shoulders
and fill the cracks of my shoes
and I am reminded that every nature video on the internet is real.
`}</Poem>
