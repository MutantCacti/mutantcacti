---
title: Shallows
date: "2024-02-28"
tags: [poetry]
description: The shallows are often far more interesting than the deeps.
---

<Poem>{`
There is great expectation of the dark;
it is well-worn and well lit.
Through glassy portholes are the deepest corals seen
and documented; in sudden light
they are revealed bone-white and stony.
By millimetre is the seabed mapped,
acutely tracked and kept in place.
For every grain of sand that quits the flat
is anomaly. The welcome
of every scient of the deep
is overstayed.
See it in the rust of reddening tanks
and pages made dark by ink
that the milky fathoms are no frontier.
Great shipwrecks are smoothed by touch.

I do not know what the shallows may possess
nor see a cause for which their trawls
may garner greater favour than below
Indeed I am not minded to see what
may be lingering in the mellow sands at all.
But nearer to the deep, we seek
not interest, nor truth.
In the shallow flecks of sunlight
are the most ravenous of pools.

Perhaps in time a braver day may come
when the shallows
being bright and cheerful and coloured
and calm
may be swum through by the sharpest minds
and revered by great explorers.
Today there are those who know already
that far above a cataclysm is a ripple
and they can see the many minds
and pools their dreaming takes
whereupon they wheel through kelpy sands,
when underburdened, mighty
and lost in understanding,
they paddle in the waves.
`}</Poem>
