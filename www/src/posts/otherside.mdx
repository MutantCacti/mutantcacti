I wrote this piece with feedback from my piano teacher at Carleton University. It was about long walks I would take through the arboretum, a large swathe of continental forest and endangered indigenous prairie.

When I listen to this song, I am immediately drawn back to that time and place. I think of sunlight, breezy grasses and crisp apples. This piece is a love letter to the world of nature I discovered in the United States, a kind of nature I have never seen elsewhere.

I experimented with multiple diffusion models using [AUTOMATIC1111's stable-diffusion-webui](https://github.com/AUTOMATIC1111/stable-diffusion-webui) to create the thumbnail locally. The artifice of the AI image makes the landscape surreal, with too many clouds and trees. Today, I would have approached the visuals very differently. The song, however, is one of the best I've ever made.
