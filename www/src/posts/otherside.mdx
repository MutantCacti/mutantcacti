I wrote this piece with feedback from my piano teacher at Carleton University. It was about long walks I would take through the arboretum, a large swathe of continental forest and endangered indigenous prairie.

I experimented with multiple diffusion models using [AUTOMATIC1111's stable-diffusion-webui](https://github.com/AUTOMATIC1111/stable-diffusion-webui) and practiced fine-tuned iteration to arrive at something I liked. I took advantage of the artifice given by the vision transformer to create a surreal landscape with too many clouds and trees.

Today, I would have approached the visuals very differently. The song, however, is one of the best I've ever made.
