I wrote this piece with feedback from my piano teacher at Carleton University. It was about long walks I would take through the arboretum, a large swathe of continental forest and endangered indigenous prairie.

When I listen to this song, I am immediately drawn back to that time and place. I think of sunlight, breezy grass and crisp apples. This piece is a love letter to the world of nature I discovered in the United States, a kind of nature I have never seen elsewhere.

The drawings in the background are my work from Field Drawing, one of the first courses I ever took at university. I will always remember the time I spent outdoors drawing with this class. It was the happiest time of my life. I believe I tasted heaven, and from that point on fury was set inside of me to try and make it real. I know I cannot drop work and sit outside drawing my whole life. So I must create a world where anybody can. Does that make sense?
