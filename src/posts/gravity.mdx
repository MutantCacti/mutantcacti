---
title: Gravity
date: "2023-01-04"
tags: [poetry]
description: The night time in the countryside.
---

<Poem>{`
As Earth is my crucifix
gravity nails
well up my blasphemy
for keeping me from the stars

you are my matthew
this one michael
archangel Sirius
first apostle in the north
flat canvas ad infinitum
for my head spinning in the grass

for the sharp-faced fat moon
bait on the fishing rod
god hangs in the ripples
of twilight on the sky

I am on my back
in the grass
looking down
at the stars
`}</Poem>
